# NGI – Nashery’s Global Initiative Website

Welcome to the official repository for the **Nashery’s Global Initiative (NGI)** website. This platform was created to serve as the digital hub for NGI's mission, values, and community engagement.

---

## 🌍 About NGI

**Nashery’s Global Initiative** is a non-profit organization dedicated to empowering communities through education, volunteerism, and sustainable development. With active members across various states and countries, NGI strives to create lasting impact through grassroots efforts and global partnerships.

---

## 📌 Project Purpose

This website is designed to:

- Provide a centralized platform for NGI members and volunteers
- Enable global membership registration and management
- Share updates about ongoing programs and initiatives
- Offer access to the NGI Volunteer Handbook and training materials
- Highlight visual stories through a professional gallery section
- Serve as a communication channel between NGI leadership and the public

---

## 🧩 Key Features

- **Membership Registration:** A simple and accessible form that allows new members to join NGI from anywhere in the world.
- **Auto ID System:** Each new member receives a unique NGI ID automatically after registration.
- **Volunteer Orientation:** Access to official training materials and the NGI handbook.
- **Events & Updates:** Stay informed with regular updates about ongoing programs and outreach activities.
- **Photo Gallery:** Visual display of NGI’s work across communities.
- **Contact Section:** Easy way to reach out to NGI or connect on social platforms.
